About Cozy Crafts

A small creative space for making things

Cozy Crafts is a small creative space for turning simple ideas into things that feel fun, personal, and a little bit special.

It started with the simple joy of designing, printing and putting little pieces together by hand. Stickers, prints, paper goods and keepsakes are the kinds of things we love making, and the kinds of things we love giving.

We're just getting started, so this shop will grow one creation at a time. Thanks for being here early.

    For building and growing the site

    Cozy Crafts design system

    Tokens, type and components used across the site. Everything is driven by the variables at the top of the stylesheet, with a matching dark theme.

    Color

    Cream paper and warm brown ink carry the page. Dusty blue comes straight from the logo and is the brand color for buttons and links. Peach and sage are small warm accents: peach marks "coming soon", sage marks "available".

    Typography

    Fraunces (soft axis on) for headings, Nunito for reading, Caveat for small handwritten notes only — never for anything longer than a line.

    Page structure

    Home Page ├─ Header (logo, nav, mobile menu) ├─ Hero Section ├─ Collections (category tiles) ├─ Featured Creations → Product Grid → Product Card ├─ Coming Soon Section ├─ About Section ├─ Why Cozy Crafts (values) ├─ Reviews (placeholder until real reviews exist) ├─ Social Media ├─ Contact Section └─ Footer Shop Page ├─ Shop status banner ├─ Category filters └─ Product Grid / Empty state Product Card ├─ Product Image (photo or concept illustration) ├─ Product Category · Product Status ├─ Product Name ├─ Product Description ├─ Product Price (or "Price coming soon") └─ Product CTA

    Adding products later

    Products, prices, photos and stock are managed in the admin dashboard at /admin and stored in Supabase. Category tiles, the featured grid, the shop, filters and the cart all update from the database. Social links live in the SOCIALS list at the top of js/store.js.
